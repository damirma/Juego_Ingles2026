REQUIRED EVIDENCE — GRAMMAR DETECTIVE AGENCY
=============================================

1. NAME OF THE GAME
--------------------
Grammar Detective Agency: The Case of the Missing Debate Trophy

2. GAME OBJECTIVE
------------------
Practice and demonstrate the correct use of "used to" and the passive voice
(present and past) by solving a mystery. Players answer grammar clues to
unlock evidence about a stolen trophy, then use everything they learned to
accuse the correct suspect.

3. INSTRUCTIONS / RULES
-------------------------
1. Split the class into 2 to 4 detective teams.
2. Read the case introduction and the list of suspects out loud.
3. Read each grammar clue out loud, one at a time, in order (8 clues total).
4. Teams try to answer using the correct grammar. Press "Reveal Answer" to
   check.
5. Award the point to the team that answered correctly (or press "No one
   got it" — the clue unlocks either way, so the case keeps moving and no
   team is stuck).
6. Press "Next Clue" to continue until all 8 clues are unlocked.
7. When the case file is complete, review the suspects and the evidence
   together, then choose who committed the theft.
8. The team that solves the case correctly wins bonus points, but everyone
   is invited to guess.

4. LIST OF GRAMMAR QUESTIONS / CHALLENGES
-------------------------------------------

CLUE 1 — USED TO (affirmative)
  Complete the sentence with 'used to': Every night, the security guard,
  Mr. Cruz, ______ (walk) past the library at exactly 9:00 PM. Last month,
  the school changed his schedule to 7:00 PM instead.

CLUE 2 — USED TO (negative)
  Rewrite in the negative form: "Diana used to leave right after her own
  team's practice."

CLUE 3 — PASSIVE VOICE, PRESENT
  Change to passive voice: "Ms. Reyes keeps all the master keys in her
  office."

CLUE 4 — PASSIVE VOICE, PRESENT
  Complete with the correct passive form: "The library doors ______
  (lock) automatically at 8:00 PM."

CLUE 5 — PASSIVE VOICE, PAST
  Change to passive voice: "Somebody moved the trophy from the front hall
  to the library last week."

CLUE 6 — PASSIVE VOICE, PAST
  Find and correct the mistake: "The security camera were turned off five
  minutes before the theft."

CLUE 7 — MIXED CHALLENGE
  Choose the correct sentence:
  A) Tom use to borrow books every Friday.
  B) Tom used to borrow books every Friday.

CLUE 8 — MIXED CHALLENGE, FINAL CLUE
  Complete the final report using BOTH a 'used to' structure and a
  passive voice structure: "Before the schedule changed, the guard
  ______ (patrol) the library at 9 PM, and that same night, the trophy
  ______ (see) for the last time on the shelf."

FINAL QUESTION — Who did it?
  Choose the culprit from the four suspects: Mr. Cruz (security guard),
  Diana (rival team captain), Ms. Reyes (school secretary), Tom (new
  exchange student).

5. ANSWER KEY
--------------

CLUE 1: used to walk
CLUE 2: Diana didn't use to leave right after her own team's practice.
CLUE 3: All the master keys are kept in her office by Ms. Reyes.
CLUE 4: are locked
CLUE 5: The trophy was moved from the front hall to the library last
        week.
CLUE 6: The security camera was turned off five minutes before the
        theft.
CLUE 7: B — Tom used to borrow books every Friday.
CLUE 8: ...the guard used to patrol the library at 9 PM, and that same
        night, the trophy was seen for the last time on the shelf.

FINAL ANSWER: Diana. She used to leave right after practice but stayed
late the night of the theft, and she knew Mr. Cruz's OLD 9 PM patrol
schedule — the same time slot he no longer covers.

7. GRAMMAR JUSTIFICATION
--------------------------
This game practices "used to" and the passive voice by requiring players
to actively produce and correct both structures in order to move the
story forward, rather than simply recognizing them. Clues 1 and 2 cover
the affirmative and negative forms of "used to" (used to + base verb,
didn't use to + base verb), which describe past habits that contrast
with the present — exactly the meaning the story depends on (a guard's
OLD schedule, a suspect's OLD routine). Clues 3–6 require transforming
active sentences into passive ones (is/are + past participle for the
present, was/were + past participle for the past) and correcting a
common subject-verb agreement mistake in the passive ("were" vs "was").
Clue 7 asks players to distinguish the correct "used to" form from a
common learner error ("use to" vs "used to"), and Clue 8, the final
clue, requires combining both a "used to" structure and a passive voice
structure in a single sentence, mirroring how these forms are actually
used together in real narration. Because every clue requires producing a
full correct sentence or spotting and fixing an error before the story
can continue, the game goes beyond memorization and tests real
understanding of both grammar topics.
