🎓 Grammar Jeopardy

Used To & Passive Voice Challenge

Set up your teams

Enter a name for each team (2 to 4 teams). Then press Start Game.

How to play (rules)
  1. Split the class into 2–4 teams.
  2. Teams take turns choosing a category and a point value from the board.
  3. Read the question out loud. The team has a short time to answer.
  4. Press "Show Answer" to reveal the correct sentence.
  5. Award the points to the team that answered correctly (or to whichever team answers correctly if the first team was wrong).
  6. The question then disappears from the board.
  7. The game ends when all 20 questions have been played. The team with the most points wins.
